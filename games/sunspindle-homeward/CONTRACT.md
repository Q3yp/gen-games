# Complete authored voyage

The engine owns lifecycle, input transport, releases and durable saves. This
generated workspace owns every scene, renderer, mechanic and route. Its source
contains the entire journey before play; no pending doorway awaits a host.

Initial state starts in Sun Court. Authored guarded routes connect the glassworks,
rain balcony and crown garden. Each safe crossing carries current player health,
records the departed room and pauses at arrival. Source initialization and each
local step preserve pending and visited destinations across rejoining.
The bird settles visibly at the garden nest only after the player arrives.
