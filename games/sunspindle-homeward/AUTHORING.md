# Homeward host contract

This is a separate, complete source workspace for an uninterrupted independent
player trial. It begins before the sunbird rescue and ends at its actual nest.
Its route and all mechanics are present before play. Do not publish changes,
queue gameplay commands, coach strategies, or reset the player during this trial.
Passive timestamped recording and read-only observation are allowed.

This generated voyage reuses authored Sunspindle code and the successful short
encounters, omitting the rejected Threadmill. Crown post-attack recovery is 1.6
seconds instead of 2.6 to reduce inert circling; no telegraph was shortened.
Preserve the original Sunspindle campaigns and their exact source releases.
A boss victory alone does not establish enjoyment or validate human reflex feel.
Record the player's voluntary stop/replay/continue judgment and any waiting.
