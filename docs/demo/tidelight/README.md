# Tidelight player-view demo

Recorded during an independent **agent-player** session on 22 October 2026. This is not human user research and not a staged developer replay.

- [29-second MP4](tidelight-player-demo.mp4): selected chronological capture bursts, with hard cuts across uncaptured intervals and a three-second final still. The original roughly five-captures-per-second timing is retained within bursts; frames are held between captures, not motion-interpolated. No soundtrack or generated imagery. The footer discloses the montage format.
- [Encounter image](tidelight-encounter.jpg): luminous swimmer in the rib-vault; the actual on-screen text reads “It curls around your hand.”
- [Home image](tidelight-home.jpg): final boat and swimmer beside the starting harbour.
- [Provenance](provenance.json): exact source filenames, recorded millisecond timestamps, selected frame durations, and SHA-256 hashes.

The still images are byte-for-byte copies of the captures. The capture tool wrote JPEG bytes to filenames ending in .png; delivery extensions are corrected to .jpg without recompression. The video is H.264, 1180 × 758, silent, approximately 1.6 MB. One padding pixel makes the encoded height even, and a disclosure is added in the pre-existing black footer outside the game viewport.

The source sequence spans 08:20:46–08:47:02 UTC. The exact timestamp of the separate final home screenshot was not supplied and is not inferred. Video selection: 0000–0011, 0024–0043, 0062–0085, 0124–0140, 0162–0223, then home.png.

This montage demonstrates observed play and its natural stopping point, not uninterrupted footage, comprehensive mechanic coverage, a standardized usability test, or evidence that every moment was enjoyable.

Final verdict: unsuccessful playtest. The player found some discoveries rewarding,
but would not choose another run of this version because too much play was slow,
unclear or repetitive. The montage preserves this iteration; it is not a claim
that the experience was consistently fun.
